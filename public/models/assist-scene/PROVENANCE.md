# Assist scene models

Runtime files for the Assist Mode scene worker (`lib/vision/scene-worker.ts`), served from `/models/assist-scene/`.

| `scene.onnx` | Screen-state classifier (15 labels in `labels.json`, input described in `metadata.json`) | Until 2026-11-04: torchvision MobileNetV3-small with ImageNet weights (`MobileNet_V3_Small_Weights.DEFAULT`, BSD-3-Clause), fine-tuned by `scripts/vision/train_scene.py` (last feature blocks and head, class-balanced, black UI theme simulated). First trained on 1,278 reviewed frames from five of the maintainer's own recordings (2026-09-21 to 09-25, brown and black UI themes; held-out 163/170). The 2026-10-02 model starts from that model's weights (`--init-onnx`) and is corrected on 5,866 frames (`scripts/vision/build_scene_dataset.py`): frames of the same recordings every 3 s and frames of recognition samples users sent under the Assist improvement opt-in (16:9, wide and near-square windows), labelled by the text on the screen where it decides the screen (3,569 frames: sell, enhance and fusion window titles, acquisition header, shop and pack selection) and by the earlier model where it was at least 0.8 sure. The frames are not distributed. Held-out frames labelled by text: 1,235/1,248 correct, 1,229 with at least 0.5 (the earlier model 1,152 and 1,108); held-out frames of the other screens: 935/935 the same label as the earlier model. The 2026-10-23 model starts from that one and is corrected on 6,341 frames: the same frames plus 546 frames (one a second) of the maintainer's recording of 2026-10-23 with the 2026-10 run start, labelled by text where it decides the screen: the starlight, start gift and observation screens and their confirmations as start_gift, the dungeon formation as battle_setup, the dungeon entry as run_setup, NOW LOADING screens as loading (one earlier frame, an old starlight confirmation, moved from run_setup to start_gift). Held-out frames labelled by text (1,318): 0.991 correct, 0.986 with at least 0.5 (the earlier model 0.984 and 0.976); held-out frames of the other screens: 986/986 the same label; on the recording's half-second frames (not trained on) the starlight, start gift, observation and loading screens are read at 0.92 to 0.98 (sha256 `897b11d5dd950471…`). The 2026-11-04 model starts from that one (6 epochs) and adds 5,062 labelled Mirror Dungeon captures of the maintainer's, labelled from the game's own UI: shop, theme pack selection (cards still sliding in), ? rooms, sell and fusion windows (an acquired-gift popup over the fusion window as gift_acquired), in Korean, English and Japanese at 1280×720 to 1920×1080 and 4:3, many of them taken while the screen was still changing, and the shop's purchase dialog (shop) and the acquired popup after a purchase (gift_acquired), which look alike. Whole capture runs are held out (1,125 frames): 1,110 → 1,125 right, 1,084 → 1,125 read with at least 0.5 (theme pack selection 160 → 180 strong, fusion 202 → 216 right). The held-out frames of the earlier data: recordings 582/589 as before, recognition samples 463 → 462 (the one, an acquired popup fading in over a reward choice, was read at 0.31 before) (sha256 `b8c503173e57f1f5…`). The 2026-11-04b model is MobileNetV4-Conv-Small instead (timm `mobilenetv4_conv_small.e2400_r224_in1k`, ImageNet weights, Apache-2.0), every layer trained 12 epochs on the same frames, with the same input (224×224 RGB, ImageNet mean and std). On the same held-out frames: the maintainer's captures 1,125/1,125 (all at 0.5 or more), recordings 582/589, recognition samples 462/463 with 461 at 0.5 or more (459 for the MobileNetV3 model). In Chrome on WebGPU a read takes 3.7 ms (3.9 ms), the file is 10.0 MB (6.1 MB) (sha256 `c2ccf0e040e5989b…`). |

| `portrait-index.json` | The formation reading's index (`lib/vision/formation-bar.ts`): every Identity's profile art, normal and uptie 3+, as the Mirror Dungeon's bottom bar shows it (the whole profile at 80% of a slot, centred), cut to a disc of the face above the HP / SP numbers at 32 px: 385 arts, 186 RGB pixels each, compared by normalised correlation. Built by the maintainer's pipeline from the game's Identity profile art. On labelled bar captures of the maintainer's (34 parties covering every Identity at uptie 4 and uptie 2 art, each at 1280×720, 1440×1080 and 1920×1080) 1,200 of 1,224 slots are named right and 24 left unnamed (a lead under 0.05), none wrong. |
| `icon-classifier.onnx` | Gift-icon classifier for fusion material slots (461 classes in `icon-labels.json`: every catalog gift, the five vestiges as `9991`, and `blank`; input 96×96 RGB crops of 1.3× the slot region, ImageNet normalization, logits out) | torchvision MobileNetV3-small with ImageNet weights, trained by `scripts/vision/train_icons.py` (12 epochs × 48,000 synthetic slots) on the site's catalog icons, grade numerals (`public/assets/ego-stages`) and keyword icons pasted into empty-slot crops, with fading, small captures, JPEG and cursor augmentation. The 2026-10-02 model starts from the 2026-09-27 model's weights (`--init-onnx`, 3 epochs at a tenth of the step) and adds the four gifts the catalog gained (9836–9839), with 2,750 empty-slot crops. Empty slots and the 1,878 labelled evaluation crops come from recognition samples users sent under the Assist improvement opt-in (`scripts/vision/build_icon_dataset.py`); they are not distributed. 1,876/1,878 labelled real slots correct, as the earlier model on the same crops (the other two are miscropped); with the true gift removed the highest score for another gift is 0.43 (earlier 0.47) (2026-10-02 sha256 `17b65752ab90ec2d…`). The 2026-10-07 model starts from the 2026-10-02 model's weights (one epoch at a tenth of the step) and adds real slot crops of the gifts real captures read weakly, dark drawings on the dark slot (9169, 9030, 9036, 9152, 9023, 9047): 26 crops labelled by eye from recognition samples, 15 times each among the 48,000 synthetic slots (`--real-train`). 56 crops of ten such gifts from other confirmation dialogs are held out: strong reads 4 → 52, top-1 55 → 56. On the 1,878 evaluation crops 1,876 are correct as before, 1,854 read strongly (1,840), and with the true gift removed the highest score for another gift is 0.57 (0.43). In the fusion sample replay no strong slot read of the earlier model changed its gift (2,319), and 139 of 174 dialogs are complete (132) (sha256 `9642c5fe95f481ba…`). The 2026-11-04 model starts from the 2026-10-07 model's weights (3 epochs at a tenth of the step) and adds the eight gifts the catalog gained (9284–9291), which the earlier model could not name. Labelled slot crops from the maintainer's own captures (shop shelf, sell and fusion owned lists, theme pack clear bonus, fusion wheel and confirmation, tooltips, acquisition; each also as a 1280-wide JPEG 75 capture gives it) train with the synthetic slots: 6,358 crops, 4 times each among the 48,000 a epoch, with 2,130 empty wheel-slot backgrounds from the same captures. A fusion window whose wheel is still empty is left out: its "select a keyword" prompt covers the owned list. 1,414 crops of other captures are held out: top-1 1,363 → 1,411 (the eight new gifts 0 → 32 of 32, owned list 440 → 449 of 450), strong reads 1,228 → 1,408 with no wrong strong read; with the true gift removed the highest score for another gift is 0.73 (0.84 for the earlier model on the same crops) (sha256 `f632534025e3ee4b…`). The 2026-11-04b model is MobileNetV4-Conv-Small instead (timm `mobilenetv4_conv_small.e2400_r224_in1k`, ImageNet weights, Apache-2.0; `--arch mnv4s`), every layer trained on the same slots, then 3 more epochs at a tenth of the step (`--init-pt`) after 61 empty wheel-slot backgrounds that showed a gift were taken out (4,819 left), with the same input (96×96, ImageNet mean and std). On the 1,414 held-out crops: top-1 1,414 (1,411), 1,411 strong reads (1,408), no wrong strong read, and no gift named strongly with the true one removed. On the 1,934 labelled crops of recognition samples no slot is read strongly as another gift (the earlier model 0 as well); its 19 other strong reads name `blank` for 18 crops labelled 9057 that show an empty wheel frame and for one dark miscrop (labels wrong, the reading right). In Chrome on WebGPU a read takes 4.5 ms (4.9 ms), also for 16 slots at once, and the file is 12.3 MB (8.0 MB) (sha256 `1a80a3f279d28168…`). |
