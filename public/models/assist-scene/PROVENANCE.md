# Assist scene models

Runtime files for the Assist Mode scene worker (`lib/vision/scene-worker.ts`), served from `/models/assist-scene/`.

| File | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `scene.onnx` | Screen-state classifier (15 labels in `labels.json`, input described in `metadata.json`) | torchvision MobileNetV3-small with ImageNet weights (`MobileNet_V3_Small_Weights.DEFAULT`, BSD-3-Clause), fine-tuned by `scripts/vision/train_scene.py` (last feature blocks and head, class-balanced, black UI theme simulated). First trained on 1,278 reviewed frames from five of the maintainer's own recordings (2026-09-21 to 09-25, brown and black UI themes; held-out 163/170). The 2026-10-02 model starts from that model's weights (`--init-onnx`) and is corrected on 5,866 frames (`scripts/vision/build_scene_dataset.py`): frames of the same recordings every 3 s and frames of recognition samples users sent under the Assist improvement opt-in (16:9, wide and near-square windows), labelled by the text on the screen where it decides the screen (3,569 frames: sell, enhance and fusion window titles, acquisition header, shop and pack selection) and by the earlier model where it was at least 0.8 sure. The frames are not distributed. Held-out frames labelled by text: 1,235/1,248 correct, 1,229 with at least 0.5 (the earlier model 1,152 and 1,108); held-out frames of the other screens: 935/935 the same label as the earlier model (sha256 `34f442b705f747a3…`). |
| `visual-encoder.onnx` | 576-dimension image encoder | The same torchvision MobileNetV3-small ImageNet weights, exported unchanged by `scripts/vision/build_embedding_index.py` |
| `embedding-index.json` | Encoder vectors of the site's own E.G.O gift and event artwork | `scripts/vision/build_embedding_index.py` |
| `icon-classifier.onnx` | Gift-icon classifier for fusion material slots (453 classes in `icon-labels.json`: every catalog gift, the five vestiges as `9991`, and `blank`; input 96×96 RGB crops of 1.3× the slot region, ImageNet normalization, logits out) | torchvision MobileNetV3-small with ImageNet weights, trained by `scripts/vision/train_icons.py` (12 epochs × 48,000 synthetic slots) on the site's catalog icons, grade numerals (`public/assets/ego-stages`) and keyword icons pasted into empty-slot crops, with fading, small captures, JPEG and cursor augmentation. The 2026-10-02 model starts from the 2026-09-27 model's weights (`--init-onnx`, 3 epochs at a tenth of the step) and adds the four gifts the catalog gained (9836–9839), with 2,750 empty-slot crops. Empty slots and the 1,878 labelled evaluation crops come from recognition samples users sent under the Assist improvement opt-in (`scripts/vision/build_icon_dataset.py`); they are not distributed. 1,876/1,878 labelled real slots correct, as the earlier model on the same crops (the other two are miscropped); with the true gift removed the highest score for another gift is 0.43 (earlier 0.47) (sha256 `17b65752ab90ec2d…`). |

Generated by `npm run build:assist-worker` (not committed): `scene-worker.js` and the onnxruntime-web (MIT) runtime files
`ort-wasm-simd-threaded.asyncify.{mjs,wasm}` copied from `node_modules`.
